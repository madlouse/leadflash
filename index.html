常见问题

FAQ

会不会让客户知道是 AI？

默认建议披露「助手先回复，师傅稍后确认」，尤其是涉及上门时间与收费。话术你审批，我们不伪装持证师傅本人。

能不能保证每个月多接多少单？

不能。我们提升的是响应速度与跟进纪律。线索量和成交价仍取决于你的流量、口碑与报价。

我们没怎么投广告，有用吗？

有。只要你已有微信咨询、电话、平台留言或公开帖互动，秒回就能减少浪费。没有流量时，我们不假装能「凭空造线索」。

和直接买一个 chatbot 有什么区别？

Chatbot 是工具；LeadFlash 是产品化会员——行业模板、规则、每周优化、人机边界。你买的是「跑起来的工作流」，不是登录账号自己摸索。

数据安全吗？

只处理你授权渠道里的咨询内容，用于回复与优化。不做非法爬取私域、不卖你的客户名单。

签约多久？怎么停？

按月。演示满意再开。停止方式在合同里写清（按月可停，具体以协议为准）。

覆盖哪些城市？

远程交付，不限城市。本地上门的服务半径由你自己的业务决定，我们帮你在话术里问清区域是否可接。

Will customers know it’s AI?

We recommend disclosing “assistant replies first; tech confirms shortly,” especially for scheduling and money. You approve scripts; we don’t impersonate a licensed contractor.

Can you guarantee more jobs per month?

No. We improve response speed and follow-up discipline. Volume and close price still depend on your traffic, reputation, and pricing.

We barely run ads — is this useful?

Yes, if you already get chats, calls, marketplace messages, or public post inquiries. We don’t pretend to conjure leads from zero traffic.

How is this different from buying a chatbot?

A chatbot is a tool. LeadFlash is a productized retainer: vertical templates, rules, weekly optimization, human boundaries — a running workflow, not a login to figure out alone.

Is data safe?

We only process inquiries on channels you authorize, for reply and optimization. No illegal private-group scraping; we don’t sell your customer list.

Contract length / cancel?

Month-to-month after a demo. Cancel terms go in the agreement.
